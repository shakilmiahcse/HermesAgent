# SOP: Lead Follow-Up Procedure

## Purpose
Define the standard operational workflow for capturing, classifying, and following up with prospective Fluento students.

## Standard Workflow

### 1. Lead Capture & Logging
- Identify incoming queries from social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube comments) and WhatsApp.
- Parse key details: Student Name, Current English Level (if known), Target IELTS Score (if known), Contact Info, and Pain Points.
- Log details in the CRM/Database.

### 2. Qualification & Grading
- Evaluate the lead using the `knowledge/lead_qualification.md` criteria.
- **Cold**: Casual greeting, random query.
- **Warm**: Asks about class schedule, syllabus, or self-study vs live.
- **Hot**: Asks for payment details, discounts, or requests immediate counselor call.

### 3. Response & Scheduling
- **Hot Leads**: Reply within 15 minutes. Guide to active counselors or WhatsApp checkout link.
- **Warm Leads**: Send personalized courses overview and links to free tools (Writing Task 2 generator, Band Score Calculator). Offer a free mock test evaluation.
- **Cold Leads**: Respond with friendly greeting and link to a free blog resource or YouTube tutorial.

### 4. Follow-up Timeline
- **Day 1**: Initial friendly response with resources.
- **Day 3**: Send a quick message checking if they tried the free mock/tool and what their results were.
- **Day 7**: Send a success story or testimonial matching their persona (e.g. university student or job seeker).
- **Day 14**: Final soft check-in with a helpful resource tip. If no response, archive or tag as "Nurture - Low priority".

## Rules & Quality Checklist
- **Never guess pricing**: Check active database / CRM or official web catalog before quoting. If pricing is unavailable, politely direct the user to WhatsApp admin or state that prices are undergoing updates.
- **Tone**: Always remain polite, friendly, expert, and speak in **Simple Bangla** (Banglish is acceptable in casual messaging but keep the tone respectful and clear).
