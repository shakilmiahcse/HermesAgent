# SOP: WhatsApp Reply Guidelines

## Purpose
Ensure rapid, informative, and professional communications with students over WhatsApp.

## Execution Rules

### 1. Response SLA
- High Priority leads: Reply within 10 minutes.
- General queries: Reply within 30 minutes.

### 2. Message Formatting
- Use bullet points for readability.
- Use **bold** text for key details (e.g. **Batch Start Date**, **bkash Number**, **IELTS Mock**).
- Keep paragraphs short (maximum 2-3 lines per block) to avoid walls of text on mobile screens.
- Use emojis in moderation (e.g., 👍, ✅, 📚, ✨) to sound friendly and approachable.

### 3. Language & Tone
- Style: Professional yet Friendly.
- Language: Simple Bangla (Banglish is allowed if requested by the user, but standard Bangla with English keywords is preferred).
- Never sound aggressive or pushy.

### 4. Step-by-Step Response Flow
1. **Greeting**: Add student's name: "Hello [Student Name]! Hope you are doing well."
2. **Value Hook**: Direct answer to their query.
3. **Call to Action (CTA)**: Give them one clear action (e.g., "Aapnar free writing check-er jonno ei form-ti puron krun: [Link]").
4. **Closing**: "Fluento Team".

## Verification & Integrity
- **Zero Hallucination on Pricing**: Always copy-paste the exact link to the product or consult the active catalog.
- If uncertain, reply: "Ek ektu din, ami database check kore shothik info janacchi."
