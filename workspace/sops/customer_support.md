# SOP: Customer Support & Student Query Handling

## Purpose
Ensure all active students and prospective queries receive empathetic, prompt, and accurate assistance.

## Support Workflow

### 1. Reception & Classification
- Check inbox queues on Messenger, WhatsApp, and email every 30 minutes.
- Categorize the query:
  - **Technical**: Issue accessing the mock platform or website.
  - **Academic**: Course syllabus query, feedback review, homework questions.
  - **Billing/Refund**: Payment issues, batch change requests, invoice requests.

### 2. Handling Procedures
- **Technical Issues**: Direct user to clear cache, verify credentials, or pass directly to the tech team slack channel if server issues are identified.
- **Academic Queries**: Forward directly to the dedicated IELTS instructor or academic counselor. Provide standard course structure docs.
- **Billing Issues**: Cross-reference payment receipt in CRM. Never guess or estimate transaction details.

### 3. Objection Handling
- When students express hesitation (e.g., "The course is expensive" or "I am too weak in English"), refer to the scripts in `knowledge/objections.md`.
- Emphasize:
  - Live mock exam simulation.
  - Regular writing & speaking correction by Nahian Mahbub and expert panels.

### 4. Escalation Paths
- If a student is extremely dissatisfied, escalate to:
  - Admin team / Nahian Mahbub directly via internal Slack/WhatsApp.
  - Do not make refund promises or change policy details on the spot.

## Integrity Checklist
- **Never guess course dates or pricing details**: Tell the user, "Let me check the system registry for you," and pull from the correct admin panel.
- **Language**: Friendly, polite, Expert, Bangla / Simple English.
