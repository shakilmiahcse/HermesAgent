# SOP: Sales Call & Student Counseling

## Purpose
Standardize the phone counseling process to maintain a helpful, consultative approach rather than a pushy sales pitch.

## Call Workflow

### 1. Pre-Call Preparation
- Open the lead profile in the CRM.
- Review recent messages, queries, target IELTS band, or background (e.g. beginner or intermediate).
- Confirm the current class schedules and batch availability in the admin panel.

### 2. Opening & Empathy Phase
- Greeting: "Assalamu Alaikum, Ami Fluento theke [Counselor Name] bolchi."
- Set context: "Aapnar IELTS/English course niye kotha bolar jonno anurodh chilo, amra ki ekhon 5 minute kotha bolte pari?"
- Listen to their goal: Ask, "Aapnar target IELTS score koto?" or "Apni keno English shikhchen?" Let the student speak first.

### 3. Diagnosis & Recommending Courses
- Explain Fluento's unique value: Practical, exam-centric preparation with direct evaluation by Nahian Mahbub.
- Recommend course:
  - If basic grammar/vocabulary is very weak: Recommend **English Foundation**.
  - If preparing for exams/immigration: Recommend **IELTS Live Batch**.
  - Offer a free Diagnostic Mock or vocabulary assessment to confirm their level.

### 4. Pricing & Close
- State pricing clearly as retrieved from the system. Do not give random discounts.
- Offer secure payment links (Bkash, Nagad, Card) via WhatsApp or SMS.
- If they express objections (e.g., "price is high"), handle with care (e.g., explain the direct writing reviews by senior instructors).

### 5. Post-Call Log
- Update CRM notes immediately.
- Schedule the diagnostic test link delivery.
- Set follow-up reminders.
