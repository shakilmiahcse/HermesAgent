# SOP: Content Publishing Workflow

## Purpose
Standardize the process of generating, reviewing, formatting, and scheduling Fluento's digital content assets to maintain consistent brand presence.

## Publishing Workflow

### 1. Topic Identification & Selection
- Pull content ideas from the prompt pipelines (`weekly_content_review.md`, `trend_detector.md`) or recent student pain points.
- Map the selected topic to one of the target customer personas (e.g. University Student, Job Seeker, Visa Applicant).

### 2. Copywriting & Scripting
- Draft scripts or social copy following the guidelines in `knowledge/tone.md`.
- Include a clear, singular **Call to Action (CTA)** (e.g., "Try our free writing generator", "Inbox us to get the live class syllabus").
- Add relevant tags and keywords (e.g., #Fluento, #IELTSBD, #IELTSPrep).

### 3. Visual & Asset Preparation
- Select appropriate brand templates from `assets/brand/`.
- Ensure logo placements align with visual guidelines.
- Video duration: Short form (< 60s) for TikTok/Reels, Long form (> 3 mins) for YouTube tutorials.

### 4. Review & Approval Checklist
- Ensure NO fabrication of facts:
  - Check that all stated IELTS band score improvements match audited success stories.
  - Verify that mock test URLs and course descriptions are accurate.
  - Ensure zero spelling or grammar errors in both English and Bangla text.

### 5. Scheduling & Distribution
- **Facebook & Instagram**: Publish daily during peak hours (e.g. 12:00 PM and 07:00 PM).
- **YouTube**: Post weekly tutorial batches (preferably Friday morning).
- **TikTok/Reels**: Post short-form tips 3 times a week.

## Analytics Logging
- Do not hardcode metrics in the files. Record monthly stats (views, reach, link clicks) in the central tracking database.
