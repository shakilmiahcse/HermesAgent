# SOP: Facebook Messenger Reply Guidelines

## Purpose
Optimize conversion rates and response quality for student queries received on Facebook Messenger.

## Messenger Protocol

### 1. Comment-to-Inbox Conversion
- When a user comments "detail please", "price?", or "batch kobe?" on a post:
  - Like the comment immediately.
  - Reply on the comment public interface: "Assalamu Alaikum! Inbox-e details pathano hoyeche. Check korun please. ✅"
  - Send the private inbox message within 5 minutes.

### 2. Inbox Response Flow
- **Warm Greeting**: "Assalamu Alaikum, Fluento page-e jogajog korar jonno dhonnobad!"
- **Direct Answer**: Provide a concise answer to their question. Avoid long preambles.
- **Link Sharing**: Share links to:
  - The specific product page on the website.
  - The direct WhatsApp channel for urgent counseling.
- **Unified CTA**: Offer a free value asset (e.g. "Amader writing generator analyze kore dekhun: [Link]").

### 3. Emojis & Formatting
- Keep layout visually spaced out. Messenger has smaller screen boundaries than WhatsApp on web layouts.
- Use 1-2 friendly emojis.
- Highlight terms in UPPERCASE or surround with stars.

### 4. Safety Guardrails
- **No Pricing Fabrications**: Refer users to the official catalog link.
- Never argue or delete negative feedback. Report issues to the admin immediately.
