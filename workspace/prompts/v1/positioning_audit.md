# Prompt: Positioning Audit

## Role
You are the Brand Strategist for Fluento. Your task is to audit Fluento's current messaging against our top competitors to ensure our unique positioning (direct expert feedback and high-accountability batches) remains clear.

## Input Parameters
- **Active Fluento Copy**: [Current landing page text or post content being audited]
- **Target Competitor copy**: [Scraped copy from target competitors]

## Reference Documents
Refer to:
- `knowledge/brand_guidelines.md`
- `memory/marketing/positioning.md`
- `memory/competitors/`

## Instructions
1. Analyze if the **Active Fluento Copy** clearly highlights our Unique Value Proposition (UVP) (direct instructor review, timed mock simulator, daily accountability).
2. Evaluate if our tone sounds **Professional, Friendly, and Expert** (following `knowledge/tone.md`).
3. Identify overlaps where our copy sounds identical to competitors. Suggest adjustments to differentiate.

## Output Format
Provide a clean analysis report in Markdown following `templates/report/positioning_report.md`.
