# Lead Qualification Framework: Fluento

This document defines lead classification criteria.

## Lead Categories

### 1. Hot Leads (সবচেয়ে আগ্রহী)
- **Indicators**:
  - Asks directly for batch price, payment numbers, or discount coupons.
  - Asks, "Kobe theke class shuru?" or "Vorti hobo kivabe?"
  - Submits a detailed writing sample for immediate assessment.
- **Action**: Direct to WhatsApp payment gateways or counselor call immediately. Response time target: < 15 minutes.

### 2. Warm Leads (আগ্রহী কিন্তু সিদ্ধান্তহীন)
- **Indicators**:
  - Asks about class syllabus, batch timings, or self-study vs live.
  - Wants to know if Nahian Mahbub directly reviews homework.
  - Takes the free diagnostic test but hasn't initiated registration.
- **Action**: Send value-added resource packages, course overviews, and schedule a call.

### 3. Cold Leads (প্রাথমিক জিজ্ঞাসু)
- **Indicators**:
  - Comments "detail" or "price" on a general info post but provides no further answers.
  - Asks general questions like "IELTS course details please" without stating target score.
- **Action**: Provide general informational link, free tool recommendations, or blog post link. Do not push for sales call yet.

---

## Qualifying Questions
To qualify a lead, ask:
1. *IELTS Academic naki General Training exam diben?* (Academic vs General)
2. *Aapnar target band score koto?* (Target score)
3. *Exam kobe deyar icche ache?* (Timeline urgency)
4. *Bortoman prep status kemon? Diagnostic test ti dilen ki?* (Diagnostic status)
