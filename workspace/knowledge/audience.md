# Target Audience Analysis: Fluento

This document outlines the target student demographic groups and learner types in Bangladesh.

## Primary Segments

### 1. University Students (higher education abroad)
- **Goals**: Target score of 6.5 or 7.0+ overall to qualify for undergraduate/postgraduate admissions in Canada, UK, USA, Australia, or Europe.
- **Pain Points**: Budget constraints, high test anxiety, struggling with essay formats (Writing Task 2), lack of partners for Speaking practice.
- **Expectations**: Interactive live lectures, peer support channels, affordable yet authentic mock feedback.

### 2. Job Seekers (corporate placement)
- **Goals**: Achieve fluency in speaking and writing for local corporates, banks, or multinationals. Often need General Training IELTS or practical Business English.
- **Pain Points**: Lack of workplace communication practice, feeling shy to speak English in meetings, poor grammar in emails.
- **Expectations**: Flexible schedules, practical case studies, and corporate speaking drills.

### 3. Visa / Immigration Applicants (express entry)
- **Goals**: Achieve high IELTS General Training scores (typically CLB 9: Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0, Speaking 7.0) for immigration programs.
- **Pain Points**: Limited study time due to full-time jobs, high stakes (need score to secure migration points), long break since school.
- **Expectations**: Result-oriented strategies, diagnostic checks, intensive weekend schedules, and precise feedback.

## Diagnostic Levels
- **Beginners**: Struggle with simple sentence structures, basic vocabulary, and suffer from high English phobia. Needs English Foundation before IELTS.
- **Intermediates**: Can read and understand basic English but get stuck around Band 5.5 to 6.0. Needs targeted exam strategies, vocabulary expansion, and correction to push to Band 7.0+.
