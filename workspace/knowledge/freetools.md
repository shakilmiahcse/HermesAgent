# Free Practice Tools: Fluento

This document lists the free utility tools provided on Fluento's online platform to assist learners and act as top-of-funnel lead generation.

## Active Tools

### 1. IELTS Writing Task 2 Generator
- **Features**: Generates randomized official Writing Task 2 prompts, includes a timed 40-minute countdown, has an active character/word counter, and offers direct submit buttons for evaluation.
- **Goal**: Drives students to try writing essays and sign up for writing correction checkups.

### 2. Reading Practice Engine
- **Features**: Interactive reading passages with timed questions, mock interface matching computer-delivered IELTS exam environments.
- **Goal**: Helps candidates self-assess reading speeds.

### 3. Speaking Practice System (Part 1 & Cue Card)
- **Features**: Interactive speaking prompts, cue cards with 1-minute prep timers, and audio recorders for self-assessment.
- **Goal**: Directs users to request certified instructor evaluation services.

### 4. IELTS Band Score Calculator
- **Features**: Input Listening and Reading raw scores (out of 40) to instantly compute the corresponding band score (for both Academic and General Training formats).
- **Goal**: Attracts organic search traffic looking for scoring formulas.

### 5. IELTS Typing Practice
- **Features**: Typing speed drills and key layout familiarization tailored for the computer-delivered exam.

### 6. VocabPix
- **Features**: Visual dictionary game reinforcing IELTS-specific vocabulary through images and contextual challenges.
