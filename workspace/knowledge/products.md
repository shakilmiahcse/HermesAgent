# Products & Service Catalog: Fluento

This file outlines the static product structures and packages. For current prices and active schedules, query the database or the official catalog.

## Product Formats

### 1. Live Batches
Intended for learners who require structured timelines, personal instruction, direct correction, and accountability.
- **English Foundation (2-Month Batch)**: Focuses on core grammar, syntax, pronunciation, and basic speaking confidence.
- **IELTS Live Preparation (4-Month Batch)**: Covers all 4 modules (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking). Includes live interactive lectures, scheduled homework, regular writing checks, speaking mocks, and standard mock exams.

### 2. Guided Self-Study Programs
A budget-friendly option designed for independent learners who prefer flexible learning hours.
- **English Foundation (Self-Study)**: Access to pre-recorded video lectures and automated grammar tests.
- **IELTS Full Course (Self-Study)**: Comprehensive study guides, pre-recorded strategy sessions, reading lists, and automated mock tests.

### 3. Evaluation & Feedback Services
Standalone checking services for students practicing outside of full batches.
- **IELTS Writing Evaluation**: Detailed grammar, coherence, vocabulary, and task response corrections with estimated band scores.
- **IELTS Speaking Mock Test**: Structured 1-on-1 speaking session mimicking official test conditions with detailed performance analysis.

### 4. Computer-Delivered IELTS Mocks
Simulator platform designed to replicate official computer-delivered IELTS tests (both Academic and General Training formats). Features realistic timers, reading formats, writing workspaces, and performance diagnostic panels.
