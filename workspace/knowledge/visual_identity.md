# Visual Identity Guidelines: Fluento

This document outlines the design standards, fonts, and assets configurations for visual elements.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Navy Blue (প্রধান নেভি ব্লু)**: Represents expert trust, professional quality, and stability.
  - HEX: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Fluento Orange (ফ্লুয়েন্টো অরেঞ্জ)**: Represents learning energy, youth, and encouragement.
  - HEX: `#EA580C` (Orange 600)

### Secondary Colors
- **Warm White / Light Gray**: Background grids and card elements.
  - HEX: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - HEX: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)

---

## Typography

### Primary Font
- **Inter** or **Outfit** (via Google Fonts): Used for English body text, interfaces, and headings.
- Clean, sans-serif, highly legible on digital screens.

### Bangla Font
- **Hind Siliguri** or **SolaimanLipi**: Used for all Bengali texts, maintaining clear character separation.

---

## Asset Directory Layout
All core graphics must reside in:
- `assets/logos/`: SVG logo files, favicon templates.
- `assets/brand/`: Banner structures, social media templates.
- `assets/screenshots/`: Competitor UI references, product interfaces.
- `assets/references/`: Inspiration layouts.
