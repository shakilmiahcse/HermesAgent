## Past Campaigns

### 1. IELTS Writing Week (আইইএলটিএস রাইটিং সপ্তাহ) - Q1 2026
- **Focus**: Free writing checks for 7 days.
- **Goal**: Drive registrations to the Writing Task 2 Generator tool.
- **Outcome**: Successfully onboarded the initial batch of live writing students.

### 2. Grammar Essentials Challenge (বেসিক গ্রামার চ্যালেঞ্জ) - Q2 2026
- **Focus**: 15-day daily grammar quizzes on the Facebook page.
- **Goal**: Build top-of-funnel leads for the English Foundation batch.
- **Outcome**: Increased page engagements and qualified beginner leads.

### 3. Speaking Simulation Launch (১-অন-১ স্পিকিং লঞ্চ) - Q4 2025
- **Focus**: Offering live, online simulated IELTS speaking sessions.
- **Goal**: Establish Fluento's mock testing capabilities.
- **Outcome**: Created stable recurring mock test service interest.
