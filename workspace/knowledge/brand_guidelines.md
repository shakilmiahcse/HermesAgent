# Brand Guidelines & Messaging System

This document outlines the core messaging framework and stylistic bounds of Fluento.

## Core Messaging Themes

### 1. "No Short-cuts, Just Strategies" (কোনো শর্টকাট নয়, শুধু সঠিক কৌশল)
- Avoid advertising IELTS as a "magic trick" or offering "Band 8 secrets in 7 days." Emphasize consistent, strategy-driven practice.

### 2. "Personal Feedback is Key" (ফিডব্যাকই আসল সমাধান)
- Highlight that passive reading or listening is only 50% of the work. Real score growth happens when an expert reviews and corrects writing and speaking errors.

### 3. "Empathetic, Simple Bangla" (সহজ ভাষায় শিক্ষা)
- Explain terms like "Paraphrasing" or "Coherence" in natural, relatable Bangla to make candidates feel understood.

## Brand Positioning Rules
- **We are NOT a test registry**: We prepare candidates, we do not register test slots officially.
- **Support Window**: Standard student support is available from 10:00 AM to 08:00 PM.
- **LMS Access**: 24/7 online portal access.
