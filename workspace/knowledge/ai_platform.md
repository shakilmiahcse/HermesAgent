# AI Growth Intelligence Platform: Hermes

## System Overview
Hermes is Fluento's proprietary AI Growth Intelligence Agent. It is designed to act as an automated brain to manage competitor monitoring, content generation, positioning audits, lead qualification, and reporting workflows.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Competitor Monitoring (চোখ - "Eyes")
- Performs scheduled web scrapes of competitor pricing pages and courses catalogs using browser tools.
- Watches social channels for marketing shifts.

### 2. Social Automation (হাত - "Hands")
- Generates context-aware, highly personalized comment and chat replies in Fluento's brand voice (Simple Bangla).
- Produces script outlines, captions, and educational content draft cards.

### 3. Analytics & Growth Briefing (মস্তিষ্ক - "Brain")
- Analyzes current student registration trends, lead status, and comments data.
- Synthesizes findings into standardized growth briefing documents every morning and week.

## Integration Protocols
- **Data Protection**: Hermes operates under a strict privacy model. Student identity and registration databases are kept secure.
- **Fact Verification**: Follows the rules in `knowledge/source_of_truth.md`. Hermes is barred from fabricating promotional pricing, course batches, or scores.
