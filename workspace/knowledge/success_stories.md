# Success Stories & Case Study Framework

This document outlines the standard layout and verification guidelines for posting student success stories. Do NOT fabricate testimonials or scores.

## Formatting Guidelines
Every published success story should follow the **STAR Framework**:
1. **S**ituation (বর্তমান অবস্থা): What was the student's starting point? (e.g. grammar errors, previous Band 5.5).
2. **T**arget (লক্ষ্য): What score did they need and why? (e.g. Band 7.0 for higher education abroad).
3. **A**ction (পদক্ষেপ): Which Fluento course did they join and how did they practice? (e.g. Writing corrections, live speaking mock feedback).
4. **R**esults (ফলাফল): The final official score sheet details.

## Verification Checklist
Before publishing:
- Ensure the student's TRF (Test Report Form) is uploaded and verified in the database.
- Confirm exact scores: Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, Overall.
- Obtain explicit student consent before displaying full name or image.

## Case Study Template
```markdown
# [Student Initials/Name] - IELTS Band [Overall Score] Success Story

- **Target Segment**: [e.g. University Student / Job Seeker]
- **Starting Level**: [e.g. Intermediate / Band 5.5]
- **Course Enrolled**: [e.g. IELTS 4-Month Live Batch]
- **Key Transformation**: [e.g. Writing score improved from 5.5 to 7.0]

## Student Testimonial
"[Student review quoted exactly from database]"
```
