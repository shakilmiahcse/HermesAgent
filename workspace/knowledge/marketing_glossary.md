# Marketing Glossary & Terms: Fluento

This document lists terminology used in Fluento's marketing operations and reports.

## Terms & Definitions

### 1. Diagnostic Test (দক্ষতা যাচাই পরীক্ষা)
- The initial evaluation a prospect takes to establish their grammar, listening, or speaking baseline score.

### 2. Live Batch (লাইভ ব্যাচ)
- Online classes led directly by instructors on fixed weekly schedules (e.g. Sat-Mon-Wed at 08:00 PM).

### 3. Guided Self-Study (গাইডেড সেলফ-স্টাডি)
- Self-paced courses accessing recorded lectures without direct, recurring live classroom sessions.

### 4. Writing Task 2 Generator (রাইটিং টাস্ক ২ জেনারেটর)
- Fluento's online tool where students practice essay writing under real IELTS constraints.

### 5. Lead Grading (লিড গ্রেডিং)
- Classification of prospective students into Cold, Warm, or Hot profiles based on query content and response time priorities.

### 6. Band Score (ব্যান্ড স্কোর)
- The official grading metric for the IELTS exam (scale 0-9).

### 7. Evaluation Mock (মূল্যায়ন মক)
- A simulated IELTS exam reviewed by certified instructors, providing breakdown scores for each module.
