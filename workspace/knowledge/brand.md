# Brand Definition: Fluento

## Overview
Fluento (ফ্লুয়েন্টো) is a specialized English language learning and IELTS training platform based in Bangladesh, dedicated to breaking down linguistic barriers for learners.

## Founding & Vision
- **Founder & Lead Instructor**: Nahian Mahbub (নাহিয়ান মাহবুব), a seasoned educator with over 10 years of experience helping candidates achieve language goals.
- **Vision**: A world where language skills are never a barrier to higher education, global careers, or personal connection.
- **Core Value Proposition**: Structured curriculum combined with honest, expert evaluation and relentless accountability.

## Target Demographics
- Bangladeshi learners, ranging from absolute beginners who struggle with basic grammar, to advanced candidates targeting Band 7.5+ in IELTS (Academic and General).

## Brand Pillars
1. **Expert Guidance**: Practical instruction focused on scoring criteria, rather than rote memorization.
2. **Honest Feedback**: Detailed evaluation of writing and speaking outputs, highlighting strengths and exact areas for improvement.
3. **Accessibility**: Quality training made available online for learners across Bangladesh, with supplementary physical centers.
4. **Technology Integration**: Harnessing tools like Writing Task 2 generators, typing systems, and AI-driven insights to speed up learning.
