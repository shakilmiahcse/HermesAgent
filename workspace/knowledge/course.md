# Course Syllabus & Structures: Fluento

This document outlines the static structure and syllabus templates for Fluento courses.

## 1. English Foundation Course
- **Duration**: 2 Months
- **Target Level**: Absolute Beginner to Intermediate
- **Delivery Model**: Live lectures or recorded modules

### Core Modules
- **Grammar & Sentence Mechanics**: Subject-verb agreement, tenses, active/passive voice, clause structures, and punctuation.
- **Vocabulary Development**: Functional vocabulary, context-based expressions, phrasal verbs, and avoiding repetitive words.
- **Pronunciation & Confidence Building**: Sound articulation, sentence stress, syllable stress, intonation, and speaking fluency exercises.
- **Practical Writing & Speaking**: Writing clear paragraphs, emails, and participating in general conversational topics.

---

## 2. IELTS Preparation Course
- **Duration**: 4 Months
- **Target Level**: Candidates requiring Academic or General Training IELTS prep
- **Delivery Model**: Live classes, guided self-study modules

### Core Modules
- **Listening Module**: Understanding accents (British, Australian, American), maps, section strategies, multiple choice, and blank fills.
- **Reading Module**: Skimming and scanning, True/False/Not Given, heading matching, summary completion. Academic vs General Training reading differences.
- **Writing Module**:
  - *Task 1 (Academic)*: Describing charts, graphs, tables, maps, and processes.
  - *Task 1 (General)*: Formal, semi-formal, and informal letter structures.
  - *Task 2*: Essay writing formats (Agree/Disagree, Discuss both views, Cause and Effect, Double Question).
- **Speaking Module**: Part 1 (Introduction), Part 2 (Cue Card description), Part 3 (Discussion). Emphasizing fluency, coherence, lexical resource, grammar, and pronunciation.
