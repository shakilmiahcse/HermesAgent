# Content Strategy Framework: Fluento

## Strategic Direction
Our content strategy focuses on "education-first value marketing." Instead of running aggressive ads, Fluento establishes authority by solving immediate student problems (vocabulary lists, grammar corrections, mock simulations) in public.

## Core Content Pillars

### 1. Strategy & Techniques (IELTS Hacks)
- **Format**: Step-by-step guides, video breakdowns (e.g., "How to get a 7.5 in Writing Task 1"), structure templates.
- **Focus**: High-quality actionable tips.

### 2. Common Errors (ভুল সংশোধন)
- **Format**: Spot-the-error quizzes, before/after corrections, pronunciation guides.
- **Focus**: Empathizing with beginner mistakes and providing friendly corrections.

### 3. Student Transformations (সফলতার গল্প)
- **Format**: Video case studies, score sheets comparisons, student video interviews.
- **Focus**: Highlighting the student's journey and effort.

### 4. Interactive Practice
- **Format**: Daily speaking cue card prompts, vocabulary challenges, writing prompt assignments using Fluento tools.
- **Focus**: Drive traffic to free platforms.

---

## Channel Mix Guidelines
- **Facebook (Posts & Groups)**: Daily updates, interactive quiz questions, post images.
- **YouTube**: In-depth strategy tutorials, reading passage solver videos (10–20 mins).
- **TikTok & Reels**: 60-second rapid tips, correcting common pronunciation mistakes.
- **WhatsApp Channel**: Push notifications for new free resources or course registrations.
