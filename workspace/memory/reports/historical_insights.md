# Memory: Reports - Historical Insights Framework

This document logs historical lessons learned from past campaign outputs and reporting cycles.

## Key Insights
1. **Response Time Correlates to Conversion**: Hot leads responded to within 10-15 minutes on WhatsApp show a 30% higher conversion rate than those answered after 2 hours.
2. **Writing Samples Generate High Trust**: Offering a free Task 2 diagnostic feedback leads directly to enrollments, as students instantly experience the value of expert review.
3. **Visual Branding in Simple Bangla**: Content using Slate/Navy background templates combined with Simple Bangla captions receives higher shares and bookmarks than hyper-formal post designs.
4. **Self-Study Upsells**: Independent learners who start with guided self-study often request live upgrades as they get closer to their exam dates.
