# Memory: Research - Market Trends Framework

This document outlines structural trends monitored by the growth system.

## Key Trends
1. **Migration Trends**: Surge in immigration applicants from Bangladesh toward Canada (Express Entry), UK, Australia, and Middle East.
2. **Computer-Delivered IELTS Shift**: Increasing preference for computer-delivered tests over traditional paper exams due to faster results (3-5 days vs 13 days) and clean typing interfaces.
3. **Short-Form Educational Content**: Rising popularity of educational tips on TikTok, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
4. **Demand for Direct Feedback**: Students are fatigued with standard video courses and seek interactive evaluation services.
