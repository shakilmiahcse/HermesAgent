### 1. Classes Schedule (ক্লাসের রুটিন)
- **Standard response**: Live courses are typically scheduled during evening hours (08:00 PM - 10:00 PM) to accommodate university students and working professionals. Detailed schedules are provided upon registration check.

### 2. Homework Check (হোমওয়ার্ক মূল্যায়ন)
- **Standard response**: Yes. Senior instructors review every writing and speaking homework submission. You receive feedback outlining exactly how to improve your score.

### 3. Payment Methods (পেমেন্ট পদ্ধতি)
- **Standard response**: We support secure mobile payments (Bkash, Nagad) and standard debit/credit cards through our official platform checkout.
