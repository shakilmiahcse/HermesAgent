# Memory: Customer - Student Pain Points Database

This document lists the main struggles, fears, and hurdles faced by English and IELTS candidates in Bangladesh.

## Core Pain Points

### 1. Writing Block (রাইটিং-এ সমস্যা)
- **Struggle**: Knowing the grammar rules but unable to structure arguments. Getting stuck at Band 5.5 in Writing Task 2.
- **Fear**: Failing the exam due to poor writing performance, requiring expensive re-registration.

### 2. Speaking Phobia (কথা বলতে ভয় পাওয়া)
- **Struggle**: Freezing or stuttering when asked to speak in English. Translating sentence-by-sentence from Bangla.
- **Fear**: Embarrassment or blanking out during the Part 2 Cue Card segment.

### 3. Reading Time Management (রিডিং-এ সময় না পাওয়া)
- **Struggle**: Understanding the passage but running out of time to read all sections. Difficulty with True/False/Not Given questions.
- **Fear**: Missing out on the final passage and scoring low overall.

### 4. Vague Feedback (অস্পষ্ট ফিডব্যাক)
- **Struggle**: Attending large coaching classes where homework is marked without explanatory comments or details.
- **Fear**: Not knowing why they are stuck at a certain level.
