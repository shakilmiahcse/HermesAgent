# Persona: Intermediate (স্কোর ৬.০ এ আটকে থাকা শিক্ষার্থী)

## Profile
- **Target Audience**: Students who possess moderate grammar structures but get stuck at Band 5.5 or 6.0 in IELTS.
- **Target Score**: IELTS Band 7.0 or 7.5+ overall.

## Frustrations & Pain Points
- **The Score Ceiling**: Repeatedly getting 5.5 or 6.0 in Writing/Speaking despite practicing regularly.
- **Vague Feedback**: Received coaching but only got general comments like "write better vocabulary" without specific suggestions.
- **Time Management**: Struggling to complete reading passages or Task 2 essays within timers.

## Marketing Hook & Strategy
- **Hook**: "Identify the exact errors keeping you stuck at Band 6.0 and push to Band 7.5."
- **Focus**: Emphasize **Fluento IELTS Live Batch** corrections, diagnostic reports, and targeted IELTS templates.
- **Recommended Channel**: In-depth strategy videos on YouTube, blog updates.
