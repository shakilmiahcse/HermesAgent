# Persona: Beginner (ইংরেজিতে দুর্বল ও প্রাথমিক শিক্ষার্থী)

## Profile
- **Target Audience**: Students, housewives, or professionals in Bangladesh who score low on grammar diagnostics and experience intense phobia when writing/speaking English.
- **Goal**: Build base confidence, master core grammar structures, and hold basic conversations.

## Frustrations & Pain Points
- **English Phobia**: Fear of speaking English or writing essays due to worry about making basic grammar errors.
- **Academic Jargon Fatigue**: Confused by complex grammar definitions.
- **Lack of safe practice space**: Worry about being judged or mocked by classmates.

## Marketing Hook & Strategy
- **Hook**: "Grammar simplified using native Bangla, building confidence step-by-step."
- **Focus**: Emphasize **English Foundation Course** live batches, supportive classroom spaces, and simple explanations.
- **Recommended Channel**: Facebook videos, TikTok/Reels common errors explanations.
