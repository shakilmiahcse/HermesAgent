# Persona: Working Professional (উন্নতি পিয়াসী চাকুরিজীবী)

## Profile
- **Target Audience**: Corporate officers, IT engineers, managers, and teachers in Bangladesh who want to upskill their English communication for global opportunities or promotions.
- **Goal**: General fluency, business communication (reports/emails), and cross-cultural conversation.

## Frustrations & Pain Points
- **Unpredictable work hours**: Often misses scheduled classes due to sudden office meetings.
- **Grammar insecurities**: Constantly double-checking email drafts with translation apps.
- **Passive learning fatigue**: Has taken online courses before but stopped due to a lack of live interactions or feedback.

## Marketing Hook & Strategy
- **Hook**: "Flexible English training designed for busy corporate schedules."
- **Focus**: Emphasize **Guided Self-Study** templates with recorded backups, and 1-on-1 counselor checkups.
- **Recommended Channel**: LinkedIn, email newsletter digests.
