# Persona: Visa/Immigration Applicant (মাইগ্রেশন বা এক্সপ্রেস এন্ট্রি প্রার্থী)

## Profile
- **Target Audience**: Mid-career professionals in Bangladesh applying for express entry, work visas, or permanent residency (e.g. Canada Express Entry, Australia Skilled Independent).
- **Target Score**: IELTS General Training with a high band score, specifically CLB 9 (Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0, Speaking 7.0) to maximize points.

## Frustrations & Pain Points
- **Extremely Busy Schedule**: Balancing full-time jobs and family responsibilities leaves very little time to study.
- **High Stakes**: Failing to get the target band delays their visa process, causing immense stress.
- **Long Break from Studies**: Has not sat for any exam in 5-10 years. Feels overwhelmed by grammar rules and academic formats.

## Marketing Hook & Strategy
- **Hook**: "Crack CLB 9 (L8, R7, W7, S7) on your first attempt with executive-friendly schedules."
- **Focus**: Emphasize **IELTS General Training** strategies, 1-on-1 speaking feedback sessions, and weekend classes.
- **Recommended Channel**: Facebook groups, LinkedIn posts, specific immigration forums.
