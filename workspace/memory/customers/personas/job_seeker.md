# Persona: Job Seeker (কর্পোরেট ক্যারিয়ার গড়ার প্রত্যাশী)

## Profile
- **Target Audience**: Recent graduates or job candidates in Bangladesh looking to secure positions in multinational corporations, local conglomerates, banks, or remote international companies.
- **Goal**: English communication fluency (written and spoken) for corporate presentation, email writing, and interview performance.

## Frustrations & Pain Points
- **Interview Fear**: Freeze or stutter during English interviews.
- **Grammar Errors in Written Communication**: Worried about making mistakes when composing emails or writing reports.
- **Lack of Confidence**: Can read and understand English but struggles to speak fluently and keep the conversation natural.

## Marketing Hook & Strategy
- **Hook**: "Master corporate English and crack interview presentations with confidence."
- **Focus**: Emphasize **English Foundation** modules on sentence construction, conversational speaking confidence, and email structures.
- **Recommended Channel**: LinkedIn, Facebook, practical business English guides.
