# Persona: University Student (উচ্চশিক্ষার স্বপ্নদর্শী)

## Profile
- **Target Audience**: Students currently enrolled in or recently graduated from universities in Bangladesh, planning to apply for higher education (undergraduate or postgraduate) abroad.
- **Target Score**: IELTS Band 6.5 or 7.0+ (with no band less than 6.0) to meet academic entry requirements.

## Frustrations & Pain Points
- **Budget constraints**: IELTS registration fee (~20,000+ BDT) is already a high cost, making them highly budget-sensitive regarding coaching fees.
- **Writing Anxiety (Task 2)**: Struggles to structure academic arguments, write cohesive essays, and keep vocabulary natural.
- **Lack of Practice Partners**: Hard to find peers who can practice speaking and correct their mistakes honestly.

## Marketing Hook & Strategy
- **Hook**: "Get real IELTS band score evaluations before taking the actual exam."
- **Focus**: Emphasize the value of Fluento's Writing Task 2 Generator and detailed, affordable evaluation reports. Show success stories of other university students who achieved 7.0+.
- **Recommended Channel**: Facebook, YouTube strategies, writing workshops.
