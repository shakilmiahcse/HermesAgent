# Memory: Business - English Learning Pedagogical Context

This document captures the educational context of learning English in Bangladesh.

## Market Context in Bangladesh
- **English Phobia**: Many students learn English grammar rules in school but remain unable to write clear sentences or speak confidently.
- **Bangla-Medium Backgrounds**: Often translate word-for-word from Bangla to English, leading to incorrect phrasal structures and unnatural collocations.
- **Speaking Hesitation**: Lack of supportive environments to practice spoken English. Fear of making mistakes in public.

## Fluento Approach
- **Grammar Simplification**: Explain sentence mechanics using simple Bangla analogies instead of memorizing abstract terms.
- **Habit Formation**: Encourage daily listening and writing drills over weekend cram sessions.
- **Guided Confidence**: Use Part 1 Speaking mocks as a low-stress entry point for beginners to start talking.
