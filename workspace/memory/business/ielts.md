# Memory: Business - IELTS Exam Specifications

This document holds core information regarding the IELTS exam standards.

## Module Structure

### 1. Listening
- **Duration**: ~30 minutes (+10 minutes transfer time for paper-based).
- **Questions**: 40 questions split across 4 sections.
- **Form**: Blank fills, multiple choice, labeling diagrams/maps.

### 2. Reading
- **Duration**: 60 minutes.
- **Questions**: 40 questions split across 3 sections.
- **Academic vs General**: Academic features complex journals; General Training features everyday texts, work advertisements, and guidelines.

### 3. Writing
- **Duration**: 60 minutes.
- **Tasks**:
  - *Task 1 (20 mins)*: Describing charts/graphs (Academic) or letter writing (General). Minimum 150 words.
  - *Task 2 (40 mins)*: Essay writing. Minimum 250 words.

### 4. Speaking
- **Duration**: 11-14 minutes.
- **Parts**: Part 1 (Introduction), Part 2 (Cue Card description), Part 3 (Discussion).

## IELTS Assessment Criteria (Band 0-9)
- **Writing**: Task Achievement, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy.
- **Speaking**: Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, Pronunciation.
