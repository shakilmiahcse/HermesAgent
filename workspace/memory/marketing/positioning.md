# Memory: Marketing - Positioning Strategy

This document details how Fluento differentiates itself in the Bangladeshi language preparation market.

## Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
Fluento stands out by focusing on **active feedback and student accountability** rather than passive pre-recorded lecture platforms.

## Positioning Highlights

### 1. Direct Instructor Review
- While competitors provide automated scores or generic templates, Fluento's senior instructors (led by Nahian Mahbub) directly evaluate student essay checks and conduct live 1-on-1 speaking mocks.

### 2. High-Accountability Batches
- Daily homework submission reviews and strict checklists ensure students stay on track and build consistent practice habits.

### 3. Exam-Centric Computer Mocks
- Simulating the exact computer-delivered IELTS environment helps reduce test-day anxiety, a major pain point for Bangladeshi students.

### 4. Empathetic Simple Bangla Instruction
- Breaking down complex structures in native, easy Bangla, rather than hyper-formal academic explanations, makes learning approachable for beginners.
