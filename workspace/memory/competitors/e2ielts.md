# Competitor Profile: E2 IELTS

## Brand
- E2 IELTS (E2 Language) is a globally recognized international test preparation platform offering professional coaching for IELTS, OET, PTE, and TOEFL.

## Pricing
- USD subscription packages, premium tutor session add-ons. Check website for current dollar rates.

## Courses
- Structured online self-paced courses, expert live webinars, and 1-on-1 private tutoring.

## AI Features
- Automated grammar feedback, pronoun scoring engines, mock assessments.

## Funnel
- Free trial account registration leading to premium dollar-denominated package enrollments.

## Free Tools
- Sample diagnostic tests, vocabulary challenges, and YouTube lesson broadcasts.

## Strength
- Globally certified NEAS content, highly professional platform structure, international expert panels.

## Weakness
- Expensive pricing for Bangladeshi students, lacks localized support or explanations in simple Bangla.

## Positioning
- Premium, high-end international test preparation platform.

## Recent Changes
- Updated platform backend to support custom OET and IELTS templates.

- Primary Website: https://e2language.com
- IELTS Specific Page: https://e2language.com/ielts
