# Competitor Profile: Banglay IELTS

## Brand
- Banglay IELTS is a local training platform focused on explaining IELTS strategies in native Bangla, operated by the Banglay IELTS and Immigration Center (BIIC).

## Pricing
- Online/offline course packages and mobile application subscriptions. Check official site for pricing updates.

## Courses
- App-based preparation courses, online strategy batches, immigration coaching.

## AI Features
- None prominently featured.

## Funnel
- YouTube tutorial video links leading to private app subscriptions and physical center admissions.

## Free Tools
- Public YouTube video tutorials, free strategy blog posts.

## Strength
- Large online audience, highly approachable instruction style in Bangla.

## Weakness
- App-centric self-learning limits direct instructor review, fewer options for advanced 1-on-1 checks.

## Positioning
- Accessible, Bangla-medium IELTS coaching for general learners.

## Recent Changes
- Launched the "Banglay IELTS Private" mobile app version.

## URLs
- Primary Website: https://banglayielts.com
