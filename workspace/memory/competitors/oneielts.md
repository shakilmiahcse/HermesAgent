# Competitor Profile: OneIELTS

## Brand
- OneIELTS is an online preparation platform targeting global IELTS candidates, with localized marketing in South Asia.

## Pricing
- Subscription-based plans or per-mock exam purchases. Retrieve active prices via Playwright search of official URLs.

## Courses
- Automated preparation courses, mock test credits, and self-study modules.

## AI Features
- Automated speech scoring (evaluating pronunciation, fluency) and writing essay grading algorithms.

## Funnel
- Free trials of AI scoring panels leading to premium subscription checkout loops.

## Free Tools
- Limited free AI checks for speaking and writing tasks.

## Strength
- Instant automated scoring, high scalability, 24/7 mock availability.

## Weakness
- Lacks direct, personalized counseling or detailed instructor feedback. Automated feedback can feel generic and non-actionable.

## Positioning
- Cost-effective, tech-driven practice tool for independent candidates.

## Recent Changes
- Updated speaking assessment engine with finer accent recognition filters.

## URLs
- Primary Website: https://oneielts.com
