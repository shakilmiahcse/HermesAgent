# Competitor Profile: IELTS Bangladesh (IELTSBD)

## Brand
- IELTS Bangladesh is a prominent hybrid (online/offline) training and mock test institution in Bangladesh.

## Pricing
- Offline/Online batch fees, unlimited mock test memberships. Retrieve active pricing from official web/social pages.

## Courses
- Offline physical campus batches (e.g. Narayanganj), online evening batches, specialized writing workshops.

## AI Features
- None prominently featured. Rely primarily on human evaluation and traditional classroom instructions.

## Funnel
- Free orientation seminars, physical center mock trials leading to batch enrollment.

## Free Tools
- Grammar sheets and downloadable strategy PDFs.

## Strength
- Physical presence, offline test center familiarity, unlimited mock testing access model.

## Weakness
- Large class sizes, lack of intensive 1-on-1 writing corrections, less focus on individual tracking.

## Positioning
- Traditional, trusted local coaching center for hybrid/offline prep candidates.

## Recent Changes
- Expanded physical campuses in major metropolitan hubs.
